## Problem and acceptance criteria

Specify freshness, intervals, timeouts, bounded output, probe cancellation/reaping, thresholds and hysteresis. Separate reporting from restart and dependency actions; test hanging and stale probes, flapping and restart-budget interaction. Coordinate BG-020 and BG-024. Choose numerical defaults with workload evidence.
