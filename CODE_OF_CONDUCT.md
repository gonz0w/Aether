# Community conduct

Aether is a hobby project for learning and careful experimentation. Help keep
issues and discussions useful and welcoming to people with different experience.

- Discuss ideas and evidence. Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information or post spam.
- Respect people's time. A report does not create a support or response deadline.
- Keep reports relevant and reproducible; move broad exploration to Discussions.
- Do not post credentials, private data or vulnerability details in public threads.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate material, lock conversations or
restrict participation when needed. GitHub's own conduct and abuse rules apply.
Use GitHub's **Report content** feature for abuse, without repeating private or
harmful material in another public thread. Security concerns use the separate
[private vulnerability route](SECURITY.md).

These guidelines cover this repository's issues, discussions and contributions.
